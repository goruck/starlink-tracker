      How to read these observations and screening rules

      ¹ Mean altitude is a Keplerian estimate from mean motion, using a 6,378.137 km Earth reference radius. It is useful for comparing element sets; it differs from instantaneous height above the ellipsoid.

      Deployment screening requires at least three independently collected, near-current solutions spanning 24 hours. GP and SupGP streams are never mixed. Future forecasts and same-epoch revisions do not count as completed progress. The default raising/lowering threshold is 2 km/day with at least 2 km cumulative change. Data older than 24 hours is flagged; these configurable heuristics are not SpaceX engineering limits.

      ² “First seen operational” records when this collector first observed CelesTrak’s + status. If already operational when monitoring began, the activation date remains unknown. Catalog transitions are bounded by observation times, not exact switch-on times. Neither altitude nor catalog status confirms customer-service activation.

      The dashboard reads its local database every minute. History begins with collection; missing past observations cannot be reconstructed from current elements. The collector runs while your user services are active, normally during your login session.
